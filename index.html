Communication and Information Dissemination Audit and Inspection

Communication Control Office (CCO)

Manuel V. Gallego Foundation Colleges, Inc.

MVGFC-QF-CCO-022
Purpose of this assessment: This checklist evaluates institutional communication practices, official messaging platforms, social media and branding compliance, information dissemination, communication documentation, and the support requirements of the concerned office. The CCO monitors communication controls and coordination; originating offices remain accountable for the accuracy and substance of their communications, while authorized signatories retain approval authority.

All fields in this assessment are optional. Offices may provide responses only to applicable questions and may leave non-applicable fields blank.

A. Office Information

Basic information about the office and assessment.

B. Official Messaging Platform Review

Review the office's use of Microsoft Teams and other official communication platforms.

C. Social Media and Branding Compliance

Review publicly accessible institutional accounts and the office's coordination with the appropriate communication and marketing units.

Publicly Accessible Pages / Accounts Reviewed

D. Information Dissemination Practices

Review how the office prepares, distributes, acknowledges, and records institutional information and announcements.

Communication Channels Currently Used

E. CCO Assistance and Support Requirements

Identify the assistance, orientation, coordination, and monitoring mechanisms needed by the concerned office.

Support / Assistance Required

F. Consolidated Audit and Inspection Findings

Record the consolidated findings of the assessment for each major area.

G. Recommended Actions

Document identified gaps, recommended actions, responsible offices, target dates, and follow-up status.

H. Acknowledgment

The undersigned acknowledge that the assessment was conducted for institutional communication monitoring, coordination, and improvement. Acknowledgment does not automatically signify agreement with every finding.

Names and dates entered above will be included in the electronic assessment record. Physical signatures may be completed on the generated PDF when required.
